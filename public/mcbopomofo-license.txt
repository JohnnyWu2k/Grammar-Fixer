Grammend's adapted Zhuyin dictionary data is derived from McBopomofo (小麥注音).
Source: https://github.com/openvanilla/McBopomofo/tree/5af9da259066dc270c5e5ae76f082636912b75c4/Source/Data
Adaptation: filtered character and phrase readings, converted Zhuyin to standard
keyboard keys, and joined occurrence counts for candidate ranking.
